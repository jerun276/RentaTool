# Escrow & AI Arbitration Module – Evidence Collection Guide (SE3110)

This directory holds test execution artifacts, logs, and screenshots required for SE3110 grading and viva presentation.

## Recommended Evidence Artifacts to Store Here:

1. **`01_owner_damage_claim_mobile.png`** – Screenshot of the owner filing a damage claim with damage photos and description.
2. **`02_ai_service_docker_logs.png`** – Terminal screenshot showing AI service processing the claim (`POST /api/v1/ai/execute-action 200 OK`).
3. **`03_admin_arbitration_desk.png`** – Web admin screenshot displaying Owner Claimed amount, AI Proposed Deduction, and calculated Renter Refund.
4. **`04_staff_approval_or_override.png`** – Screenshot of the adjudication modal confirming payout distribution.
5. **`escrow_test_execution.log`** – Saved terminal output from running `python test_escrow.py`.
