# Catalog Module – Evidence Collection Guide (SE3110)

This directory holds test execution artifacts, logs, and screenshots required for SE3110 grading and viva presentation.

## Recommended Evidence Artifacts to Store Here:

1. **`01_catalog_browse_mobile.png`** – Screenshot of the equipment listings feed on the mobile application.
2. **`02_category_filter.png`** – Screenshot of filtered catalog results (e.g. Generators & Power).
3. **`03_equipment_details_modal.png`** – Screenshot of equipment details, owner verification badge, and specifications modal.
4. **`04_equipment_deletion_safety.png`** – Screenshot/log demonstrating equipment deletion validation.
5. **`catalog_test_execution.log`** – Saved terminal output from running `python test_catalog.py`.
